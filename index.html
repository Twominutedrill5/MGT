Our local tavern. Just done right.

Maple Glen Tavern has been the corner spot on Limekiln Pike for [number] years. Neighbors come in after work, stay for the second half, and know the bartender by name.

The kitchen keeps it simple on purpose. The roast beef is cooked in-house and sliced thin. Steaks go on Amoroso rolls, and the long hots are the real thing.
